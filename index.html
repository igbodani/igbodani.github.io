Multimodal AICompleted pilot

Visual-memory reliability

When should a visual-memory system answer an object-location question, and when should it decline? I built an offline evaluation pipeline around DynaMem using SigLIP and YOLOE, replaying recorded observations for 120 queries across two DynaBench scenes. I compared answer filters based on observation age and age plus similarity, keeping the underlying models fixed.

Personal research project · Completed October 2026

Pilot findings & limitations

Of 120 queries, 54 were used for development and 66 were held out. A retrospective comparison used the 42 held-out queries with known labels.

At 18 answers each, the age-plus-similarity rule had 1 geometric error versus 2 for age-only. This difference depended on how tied candidates were ordered; another valid ordering eliminated it. The pilot does not establish a robust improvement or reproduce the DynaMem paper’s verified setup.

I also examined frame-cutoff sensitivity and cases where location-based scoring can conceal a wrong-object match. Queries with unknown labels were excluded from correctness scoring.
